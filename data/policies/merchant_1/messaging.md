# Customer messaging policy

How Mekong Noodle House may contact customers by text and email. These
rules exist because marketing texts are regulated and because the
marketplaces do not share customer contact details with us.

## Who may receive marketing texts

A marketing text may only go to a customer who has verified SMS consent on
file, meaning they texted a keyword to our number or ticked the box on the
Square Online checkout and confirmed. Implied consent, meaning the customer
gave us a phone number when ordering but never opted in, is not enough for
marketing.

Implied consent does allow transactional texts: order ready, order delayed,
catering confirmation. Nothing promotional may be added to a transactional
text.

Anyone who has unsubscribed is never texted again for any reason, including
transactional messages, until they opt in again.

## Quiet hours

No text of any kind between 8pm and 9am in the customer's local time. For
customers without a known local time, use Central time.

## Frequency

At most four marketing texts to any one customer in a calendar month. Email
has no frequency cap but every email must carry an unsubscribe link.

## Offers in messages

A discount offered by text or email may not exceed 15% or $10, whichever is
lower, without the owner's approval. Offers must state an expiry date no
more than 14 days out and must say "one per customer".

## Required wording

Every marketing text must end with "Reply STOP to opt out". Every message
must name the restaurant.

## Audience size

A single campaign may go to at most 500 customers without owner approval.
Campaigns to customers who have not ordered in more than 180 days need owner
approval regardless of size.
