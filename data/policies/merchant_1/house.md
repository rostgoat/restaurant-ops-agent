# House policy

Rules for staff at Mekong Noodle House on comps, refunds, discounts, and
catering. "Manager" means a shift manager on duty. "Owner" means Linh.

## Comps

A manager may comp up to $25 on a single ticket to resolve a complaint
about food quality or a long wait. Anything above $25 needs the owner, who
can be reached by text during service.

Comps for friends, family, or staff are not permitted at any amount. Staff
meals are handled separately under the staff meal rule below.

Total comps in a week should stay under 1% of that week's gross sales. If
a week runs over, the owner reviews every comp from that week.

## Refunds on in-store and online orders

A customer with a receipt may have an in-store or Square Online order
refunded to the original payment method within 7 days of purchase. Cash
orders are refunded in cash; card orders are refunded to the card and never
in cash.

Without a receipt, offer a remake or a credit for the same amount, not a
refund. Credits expire after 60 days.

Orders placed through a marketplace are never refunded by us directly; see
the marketplace terms.

## Discounts

Staff may apply the standard discounts on the register: 10% for students
and seniors with ID, and 15% for first responders in uniform. Any discount
above 15% on a single order needs a manager. Any discount above 30% needs
the owner, including on catering.

Discounts are not combined. The largest applicable discount applies.

## Staff meals

Each staff member on a shift of five hours or more may have one meal from
the Noodle Soups, Rice Dishes, or Sandwiches categories at no charge, rung
in as a staff meal, not a comp. Specialty drinks and catering items are not
included.

## Catering

Catering orders need 48 hours' notice and a 25% deposit at the time of
order. A catering order cancelled more than 24 hours before pickup gets the
deposit back. Cancelled within 24 hours, the deposit is kept. Catering
orders over $500 need the owner to confirm the kitchen can take them.

## Allergies

Any order noting a nut, shellfish, or gluten allergy is made by the lead
cook and checked by a manager before it leaves the kitchen. We do not
guarantee any dish is free of these allergens and staff must say so when
asked.
