# Marketplace terms

Mekong Noodle House lists on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. These are the
terms we operate under across the three platforms. Where a platform differs,
it is called out. Manager-level staff may act on anything here. Anything
marked "owner" needs Linh.

## Commission and pricing

DoorDash takes 25% of the menu subtotal on delivery orders and 15% on pickup.
Uber Eats takes 30% on delivery and 15% on pickup. Grubhub takes 20% on
delivery plus a 5% marketing fee, and 10% on pickup.

Menu prices on the marketplaces may be set up to 25% above our in-store
price to offset commission. They may not be set higher than that, and the
same item must carry the same price on all three platforms. Price changes go
through the owner.

## Missing or incorrect items

When a customer reports a missing or incorrect item, the platform refunds the
customer directly and charges the cost back to us by default.

We can dispute the chargeback if we have a packing photo showing the item in
the bag. Photos are taken at the pass for every marketplace order over $40.
Dispute windows: DoorDash 7 days from the order date, Uber Eats 30 days,
Grubhub 14 days. A dispute filed after the window is rejected automatically.

If the order was over $40 and no packing photo exists, do not dispute; accept
the chargeback and log it as a packing miss.

## Late, cold, or damaged deliveries

Delays and damage after the courier picks up are the platform's cost, not
ours. If a customer complains to us about a late or cold delivery, refer them
to the platform's support and do not issue a refund or a credit from our
side. The exception is a Grubhub order where we marked it ready more than 10
minutes before it actually was; that is counted as our delay and we accept
the chargeback.

## Cancellations

An order cancelled by the customer before we confirm it costs nothing. An
order cancelled after we confirm it is paid to us in full by the platform,
except on Uber Eats, where orders cancelled within 2 minutes of confirmation
are not paid.

If we cancel an order because we are out of an item, we are not paid and the
platform may lower our ranking. Offer a substitution first; cancel only when
the customer declines.

## Promotions

Platform promotions we fund ourselves (for example "$5 off orders over $30")
need owner approval if the expected cost is over $200 in a calendar month.
Promotions funded by the platform need no approval. Never run a funded
promotion on Grubhub, where the marketing fee already applies.

## Repeat disputes

A customer with three or more refunded marketplace orders in the last 90
days is flagged. For flagged customers, every missing-item claim is disputed
regardless of order value, and the owner is told.
