# Definitions

## 1. Scope

How Linh wants the words in her questions read. When a question uses one
of these words, the meaning here is the one that applies.

## 2. Lunch

Lunch is 11:00 to 13:59 local time, every day, on every channel.

## 3. The Lunch 20 Promotion

Lunch 20 ran from August 3 to August 30, 2026, during lunch, in store and
on Square Online: 20% off the order. A promo order is a completed order
carrying the Lunch 20 discount. The promotion's window is those dates and
hours on those two channels, and it is judged against the same dates,
hours and channels in 2025. The promotion brought a customer in when their
first order ever was a promo order.

## 4. Regulars

A regular has three or more completed orders in three months. Summer is
June, July and August. A regular we lost over the summer had three or
more completed orders in March, April and May and has not ordered since
the end of August.

## 5. Marketplaces

The marketplaces are DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub. Square Online
delivery is our own and is not one. Commission rates are in Marketplace
Terms §2. Prices on the marketplaces are 25% above the in-store price, so
the unit price on a marketplace order already includes the markup.
