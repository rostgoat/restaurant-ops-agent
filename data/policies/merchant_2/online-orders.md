# Online ordering and loyalty

How Ridgeline Coffee handles Square Online orders, the loyalty program, and
gift cards. We do not list on any delivery marketplace; anyone asking about
DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub should be told we are not on them.

## Pickup orders

Online orders are prepared in the order they arrive and marked ready in
the app. Peak mornings run 10 to 15 minutes from order to ready; the app
shows the current estimate and customers are told to trust it.

An order not collected within 30 minutes of being marked ready is discarded
and charged in full. If the order was marked ready late by more than 10
minutes, the customer is not charged and may have it remade.

Drinks that were wrong are remade on the spot. We do not refund an online
order once the customer has taken it from the counter, except under the
48-hour refund rule in the house policy.

## Loyalty program

Customers earn one stamp per drink purchased, in store or online, when they
give their phone number at checkout. Nine stamps earn a free drink of any
size from Signature Drinks, House Favorites, or Matcha. Seasonal Specials,
Flights, and Bubble Tea are excluded from the free drink.

Stamps expire 12 months after they are earned. A free drink reward expires
60 days after it is issued. Stamps are never transferred between phone
numbers.

Giving a phone number for loyalty is not consent to marketing texts; see
the messaging policy.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in any amount from $10 to $200 and never expire. They
cannot be redeemed for cash, and a card with a remaining balance under $5
may be cashed out at the customer's request where state law requires it.
Lost cards are replaced only if the original purchase receipt is shown.

## Order problems we cannot fix online

Allergy notes, custom milk ratios, and extra-hot requests placed through
the app are best-effort. If an order comes out wrong because of a note the
app did not pass through, it is remade and not charged.
