# Definitions

## 1. Scope

How Dana wants the words in questions read. When a question uses one of
these words, the meaning here is the one that applies.

## 2. Regulars

A regular comes in about weekly: four or more completed orders in a month.
Summer is June, July and August. A regular we lost over the summer had
four or more completed orders in May and has not ordered since the end of
August.

## 3. Seasonal Specials

Seasonal Specials are on the menu from September 1 to December 31 and off
it the rest of the year. The launch is September 1. Comparing before and
after the launch means the four weeks after September 1 against the four
weeks before it. The special worth keeping all year is the one that earned
the most total margin over last season, September to December 2025.

## 4. Drinks

The drink categories are Signature Drinks, House Favorites, Matcha,
Seasonal Specials, Bubble Tea and Flights. Pastries, Cold Case,
Merchandise and Catering are not drinks.

## 5. Free Drinks

A free loyalty drink is an order carrying the Free Drink Reward discount.
The discount amount is the drink's menu price.
