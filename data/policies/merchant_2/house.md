# House policy

Rules for staff at Ridgeline Coffee on remakes, refunds, comps, discounts,
and catering. "Lead" means the shift lead. "Owner" means Dana.

## Remakes and comps

Any barista may remake a drink the customer is unhappy with, no questions
asked, and may comp a drink or pastry up to $10 to fix a mistake we made.
A lead may comp up to $20 on a single order. Anything above $20 needs the
owner.

Comps are for our mistakes and for service problems. We do not comp for
friends or family. Staff drinks are free during a shift and are rung in as
staff drinks, not comps.

Comps for the week should stay under 2% of the week's gross sales. The
owner gets a weekly comp report either way.

## Refunds

A customer who is unhappy and still here gets a remake first. If they would
rather have their money back, refund to the original payment method the
same day. After the customer has left, a refund is available within 48
hours with the receipt or the order number from the Square Online
confirmation. Card refunds go back to the card; we do not give cash for
card payments.

No refunds on merchandise once opened, or on flights once the first cup
has been poured.

## Discounts

Standard discounts: 10% for students with ID, 10% for hospital staff with a
badge, and the loyalty reward of one free drink after nine stamps. A free
loyalty drink is not a discount and may be combined with a standard
discount on the rest of the order.

Any other discount above 10% needs a lead. Any discount above 20% needs the
owner. Seasonal Specials are never discounted.

## Online orders

Square Online orders are pickup only. An order not collected within 30
minutes of its ready time is discarded and charged in full. If we were
late making it, the customer is not charged and the order is remade on
request.

## Catering

Catering orders need 72 hours' notice and a 50% deposit. The deposit is
refundable up to 48 hours before pickup and kept after that. Catering orders
over $300 need the owner's confirmation. We do not deliver catering; it is
pickup only.

## Allergies

We cannot guarantee any drink or pastry is free of dairy, nuts, or gluten
and staff must say so when asked. Oat and almond milk are prepared on the
same equipment as dairy.
